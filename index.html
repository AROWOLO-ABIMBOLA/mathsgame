    Meet the maker

    Dr. Arowolo Ayoola

    Dr. Arowolo Ayoola is a mathematician. That means he is a grown-up who loves numbers and uses them to solve puzzles every day!

    He started a company called Data-Lead Africa. His team counts things that really matter, like how many children go to school and how many people visit the hospital. Then they use maths to find clever ways to make everyday life better for families.

    He made Number Buddies so that you can grow up loving maths too. Who knows? One day you might use numbers to help the whole world!
